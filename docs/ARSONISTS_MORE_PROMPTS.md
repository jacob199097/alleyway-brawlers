# More Arsonists ideas: the Spread

The starter deck sets fires and cashes them in. These cards fill the gaps it still has:

- **No tokens.** Embers are small throwaway fires: tributes for Heavies and something to KO for value.
- **No Continuous or Equip cards.** A slow burn that works every turn, and a fuel can to carry.
- **One Heavy.** Inferno Ike is a second big finisher.
- **Card draw.** Only Soot draws. Controlled Burn and the Ash Collector refill the hand.
- **Defence.** Fire Escape saves a character and burns the attacker.

Every effect uses blocks the Forge already has (BURN, STOKE, CLEANSE, tokens, Equip, Continuous,
recover, draw). Stats follow the starter's curve; press **Test this card in 200 CPU games** on each one
and adjust.

## Using the prompts (ChatGPT)

Same rules as the starter (docs/ARSONISTS_STARTER_PROMPTS.md):

- Attach one or two finished cards as the style reference. Use Arsonists cards **other than Cinder**,
  or cards from another clan. The reference is for style only.
- Every picture keeps the clan's colours: deep black and charcoal, blazing orange and ember-red fire,
  glowing embers, drifting ash and smoke.
- Nothing explosive. ChatGPT refuses bombs, detonators and charges, so the prompts stick to fire,
  fuel, sparks and smoke.
- Pictures are square (1024 × 1024). The Forge crops them into the card's art window.
