# Pirates starter deck: cards and art prompts

The Pirates take what's theirs. **PLUNDER** is their own keyword, like STOKE for the Arsonists and STASIS
for Nebula: it takes a card from the opponent and makes it yours. It can take a random card from
their hand (or one you pick after looking), the top of their deck, a card from their Gutter, or one of
their characters, which fights for you from then on. The deck fills its hand with stolen cards, then
cashes in with cards that count your hand ("+100 ATK for each card in your hand", "if your hand has 5+
cards"). The Fence turns spare cards into Authority.

**Against other clans:** PLUNDER from the Gutter takes a card before it can rise (UNDEAD) or use its
Gutter ability, so it hits the Underworld hardest. PLUNDER from the hand takes Wildcard's and Militia's
Hustles before they're played. A stolen Nebula or Arsonists card plays by its own text for you.

A crew of harbour thieves, canal smugglers and dock-gang brawlers working the city's waterfront at
night. Think modern city docks, not old sailing ships: container yards, fish markets, ferries,
drawbridges, flooded tunnels and rusting trawlers, with a few old-pirate touches (hooks, cutlasses,
tricorn hats, a tattered black flag).

**The deck (40):** 5 Striver lines (LV1 ×2 each; LV2 and LV3 ×3 each in the Hideout), 9 characters,
1 Heavy, 6 Hustles and Ambushes. That's 30 characters and 10 Hustles/Ambushes, like the other starters.

## Setting it up in the Card Forge

1. Make the cards in **Card Forge 2** (it has its own art storage; card numbers from 501).
2. Settings: add the clan **Pirates**, tag **PIRA**, colour **#2ec4b6** (sea teal). The name has to
   contain "Pirate": that's what unlocks PLUNDER in the Do list.
3. Clan art style: *deep navy night sea and black water, sea-teal and verdigris green, rusted iron
   orange, glints of brass and gold coins, drifting harbour fog.*
4. PLUNDER is under **Do**. Pick **From**: their hand, the top of their deck, their Gutter, or their
   field. For the hand or Gutter, tick "choose" to pick the card (a hand left unticked gives a random
   card). Set **Cards** to how many.
5. Press **Test this card in 200 CPU games** on each one and adjust. The CPU plays PLUNDER and Gutter
   abilities.
6. New art goes through the Real-ESRGAN smoother before export, like every card.

## Using the prompts (ChatGPT)

- **The clan's colours:** every Pirates picture keeps the same scheme, like the other clans: deep
  navy night sea and black water, sea-teal and verdigris green, rusted iron orange, glints of brass
  and gold, drifting harbour fog. (The Forge's Art prompt panel adds it too, from the clan's "Art
  style".)
- **No cannons, gunpowder or bombs:** ChatGPT won't draw explosives. Use hooks, cutlasses, harpoons,
  chains, boathooks, lanterns, nets and rope.
- **The reference image is for the art style only.** Don't attach Captain Marlow: ChatGPT copies
  his coat, hook and hat. Attach one or two finished cards from another clan, and later, finished
  Pirates cards other than Marlow.
- **Don't use Nebula cards as the reference:** ChatGPT copies their purple and magenta lighting as
  well as the drawing style. Militia or Underworld cards are closer to the Pirates' colours. Every
  prompt also asks for teal and orange light with no purple. If a picture still comes out purple,
  follow up with: "Keep everything exactly the same, but change all the purple and magenta light to
  cold teal moonlight and warm sodium-orange dock lamps."
- **LV2 and LV3:** attach only that character's LV1 picture (it's in the same style), so the face and
  build carry over and nothing else does.
- Every picture is square (1024 × 1024): the Forge crops it into the card's art window.
- Not every card has to be a person: creatures and monsters fit too (The Leviathan is a sea serpent).
- Each character has its own look, weapon, pose and setting on purpose. Only Marlow wears a captain's
  coat and tricorn; nobody else has a hook for a hand.
- **Strivers:** all three levels share one flavour text (and its "by").

## Underworld: Gutter abilities

The Underworld cards can now have a **Gutter ability**. In the Forge, pick the trigger "In your Gutter
(Underworld: the player uses it from the Gutter, once per turn)". It needs a cost, or it's free: pay
Authority, pay Morale, discard, sacrifice one of your characters, EXILE other cards from your Gutter,
or **EXILE this card** (it leaves the game to do it). In a duel your Gutter glows teal when one is
ready, and the player clicks it and presses USE.

A few to make:
- **Restless Shade** (Common, 2 Authority, 700 / 500): IN YOUR GUTTER (once per turn, EXILE this card):
  your opponent loses 300 Morale.
- **The Ferryman** (Rare, 5 Authority, 1800 / 1600): IN YOUR GUTTER (once per turn, pay 3 Authority):
  put the most recent character from your Gutter onto the field in DEF position. (It can bring itself
  back.)
- **Bone Collector** (Uncommon, 3 Authority, 1200 / 1000): IN YOUR GUTTER (once per turn, EXILE 2 other
  cards from your Gutter): draw 1 card.
- **Wailing Widow** (Uncommon, 4 Authority, 1500 / 900): IN YOUR GUTTER (once per turn, discard 1
  card): give all your characters +300 ATK until the end of the turn.

The Pirates' PLUNDER from the Gutter (and Gil the Grave Robber) is the counter: it takes these cards
before they can be used.
